honey bee communication

Honey bees communicate through a combination of chemical signals, dances, and vibrations. The most famous form of communication is the "waggle dance," where worker bees convey information about the location of food sources to their hive mates.

honey bee products

Honey bees produce a variety of valuable products, including honey, beeswax, royal jelly, and propolis. Honey is a natural sweetener, while beeswax is used in candles and cosmetics. Royal jelly is a nutrient-rich substance fed to queen bees, and propolis has antimicrobial properties.

honey bee conservation

Honey bee populations face threats from habitat loss, pesticide exposure, diseases, and climate change. Conservation efforts focus on creating pollinator-friendly habitats, reducing pesticide use, and promoting sustainable beekeeping practices to ensure the survival of these vital insects.
