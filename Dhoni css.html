Mahendra Singh Dhoni is one of the greatest cricketers in the world. He is famous for his calm nature, excellent wicketkeeping, powerful batting, and outstanding captaincy. Millions of cricket fans admire him for his leadership and finishing skills.

About

MS Dhoni was born on 7 July 1981 in Ranchi, Jharkhand. He started his international cricket career in 2004. Under his captaincy, India won the 2007 ICC T20 World Cup, the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, and the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy. He is known as one of the greatest captains in cricket history and is respected around the world.
